          Throne of Shirvan

          A medieval Caspian economics game, c. 1100–1500

          You are the Shirvanshah, ruler of a Caspian-coast kingdom astride the Silk Road. Year by year, petitioners will reach your throne. Each will ask for something, and each decision will shift the kingdom. Keep five things in balance and your reign endures. Lose any one of them and it ends.

          The game cannot run from a file opened straight off the disk. Close this tab and double-click play.cmd in the game's folder.

          How to play

          The reign

          Each card is one year. A petitioner asks, you give one of two answers, and the five stats move. Reach the final year and the reign ends on your terms.

          The five stats

            A stat at 0 ends the reign. So does a stat at 100: a vault too full draws raiders, and an army too strong stops taking orders. Below 15 or above 85 a crisis can interrupt the year.

            The court

            Nine courtiers remember how you answer them. After each choice the card says who approved and who did not. Carry one of them far enough either way and they come back with something larger.

            Ambitions

            Each reign opens with three ambitions. They are optional. Each one met adds to the reign score.

            The Codex

            Every card teaches one idea from economics. The Codex keeps the ideas you have met and the endings you have reached, from one reign to the next.
